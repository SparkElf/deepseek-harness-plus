# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DeepSeek Harness Plus welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use respectful, constructive language.
- Welcome different viewpoints and seek clarification before assuming intent.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, personal data, or private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, doxxing, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub security advisory for this repository. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
